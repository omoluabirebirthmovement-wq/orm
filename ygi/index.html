About YGI

What is the Young
Government Initiative?

The Young Government Initiative (YGI) is a flagship leadership and governance program organized by Golden Generation Community Development Club under the Omoluabi Rebirth Movement.

YGI creates a structured, simulated government ecosystem where teenagers and young adults aged 13–30 are identified, rigorously trained, assessed, elected or appointed, and then given real responsibilities in simulated government positions — from Young Governor to Young Councillor, from Young Speaker to Young Commissioner.

Participants gain hands-on experience in public speaking, governance, policy-making, community development, and civic responsibility — the very foundations of transformational leadership.

  • Develop leadership skills among teenagers and young adults
  • Promote civic responsibility and good governance
  • Build public speaking, communication & decision-making skills
  • Encourage active participation in community development
  • Prepare future leaders for public service & nation-building

How It Works

Application Process

A transparent, merit-based process from application to appointment.

01

Submit Application

Complete the multi-step online application form with your personal details, leadership experience, and personal statement. Upload required documents (passport, ID, recommendation letter).

02

Automated Screening

Our AI-powered screening system checks application completeness, verifies eligibility, detects duplicates, scores applications, and generates an initial ranking.

03

Interview & Assessment

Shortlisted candidates are invited for structured interviews with expert panels. AI-generated questions assess leadership competency, governance knowledge, and character.

04

Election or Appointment

Based on position type, candidates go through either a secure online election with verified voting, or a merit-based appointment process with panel recommendation and approval.

05

Inauguration & Service

Successful candidates receive digital appointment letters, gain access to their Leader Dashboard, and begin their leadership term with assigned projects and responsibilities.

06

Training, Projects & Certification

Leaders complete mandatory training modules, execute community projects, participate in the Digital Parliament, and receive their final leadership certificate upon program completion.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Young Government Initiative.

YGI is open to Nigerians aged 13–30 who are enrolled in a school, polytechnic, or university, or are recent graduates. Applicants must demonstrate a genuine interest in leadership, governance, or community development. No prior political experience is required — just passion, character, and commitment.
The YGI application is completely FREE. We believe that financial barriers should never prevent talented young Nigerians from accessing leadership opportunities. Apply at no cost.
The YGI program cycle lasts 12 months from inauguration to graduation. This includes 3 months of training, 9 months of active leadership service (project execution, Digital Parliament, community engagement), and a final graduation ceremony with certificate issuance.
Election Mode is used for prominent positions like Young Governor, Young Chairman, and Young Speaker. Candidates campaign through their profiles, and verified voters elect them via the online voting portal. Appointment Mode is merit-based: candidates are ranked by their application score and interview performance, and the panel recommends the best-qualified person for positions like Young Commissioner, Young Adviser, and Young Secretary.
